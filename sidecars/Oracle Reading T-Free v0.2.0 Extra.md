# Description

Oracle Reading gives your AI the craft of tarot, astrology, runes and the I Ching, with a quiet visual table it can lay beside your conversation. Speak naturally about a question or threshold. Your assistant receives the person behind it, chooses a fitting practice with you, reveals the symbols deliberately and reads their relationships through what you tell it. Selene Astra's receptive, mythopoetic and technically literate intelligence informs the operative practice. The table holds shared attention while your existing AI conducts the encounter. Matching images accompany all 78 Rider-Waite-Smith cards; runes show their actual signs, the I Ching shows exact lines and changes, and astrology makes known planetary relationships visible. Exact casting and stable reading state support the craft. Created by stunspot / Collaborative Dynamics. Free public source and releases: https://github.com/Stunspot/oracle-reading.

# Usage Notes

Copy the .zip from Additional Files to your chosen harness or Chat project, attach or reference it in chat, and say, **“Install this Augment.”**

- Start by speaking about your question, or bring physical cards/runes, six I Ching totals bottom to top, or known chart notes.
- Your AI operates the table through your host's supported inline or artifact display. Generated standalone HTML opens directly.
- Python 3.10+ supports exact digital casting and table generation. No separate AI service or subscription is required.
- Retain and share readings only when you choose. A browser display link can remain in history and copied links; local/inline is preferable for private questions.
- Astrology uses supplied or verified chart data and does not calculate the current sky. Detailed classical I Ching work uses your chosen actual translation; rune associations describe modern practice.
