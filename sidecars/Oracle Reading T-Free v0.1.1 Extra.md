# Description

Oracle Reading gives your assistant the craft of tarot, astrology, runes and the I Ching, with a welcoming reading studio you can use on your own. Bring a question, cast digitally or enter a physical draw, explore the symbols and keep a private journal when you choose. The assistant skill goes deeper through listening, contextual synthesis and tradition-specific knowledge while preserving your own judgment. The original reference library covers 78 RWS-style tarot cards, 24 Elder Futhark runes and all 64 King Wen figures; astrology interprets known placements you supply. The same cast travels into your chosen AI through a complete reading brief. Created by stunspot / Collaborative Dynamics. Free public source and releases: https://github.com/Stunspot/oracle-reading.

# Usage Notes

Copy the .zip from Additional Files to your chosen harness or Chat project, attach or reference it in chat, and say, **“Install this Augment.”**

- Start with a question, your physical cards/runes, six I Ching coin totals from bottom to top, or known chart placements.
- Open the complete extracted studio with Launch Oracle Reading.cmd on Windows (Python 3.10+) or python serve.py on other platforms.
- Save readings only when you want them in this browser. Export important entries and review question/reflection text before sharing.
- Deepen with AI copies a brief for your assistant. The studio itself uses original commentary and reflection prompts.
- Astrology does not calculate charts or the current sky. Detailed classical I Ching line reading uses your chosen translation; rune associations describe modern practice.
